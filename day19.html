Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

A watch sheet is data. Today you turn data into rules you can use. Look across your rows for patterns: where does the horizon sit when Paul is powerless vs powerful? When does color drain away (Giedi Prime) and why?

The infrared arena is a broken rule worth stealing in spirit: changing the whole image texture says "this is a different world with different rules". Your twist could use texture the same way: the machine's road could be slightly too clean (see the A-to-Z color script, stage D).

Worked example

From row to rule

Row: "Arena, black-and-white, harsh sun, crowd as pattern." Pattern: texture change = world change. Rule for your film: "The machine's road has no grain and a sun that never moves; the real kitchen has grain and a light that climbs the wall." Read the full version in the A-to-Z worked example (stage D, color script).

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Read all your rows. Highlight horizon notes in one color, want notes in another.
  2. Write three rules you learned (one about horizon, one about color, one about wants).
  3. Apply each rule to The Experience Machine: one change to the road, one to the kitchen, one to the twist.
  4. Update your 10-second shot design table from Day 14 with those changes.
What good looks like
  • Three rules, each traced back to specific rows.
  • Concrete changes (for example 'horizon even lower in shot 1, 80% sky') rather than vague ones ('make it more epic').
  • An updated shot table.
