Beats are the moments where something turns. A short needs fewer than a feature, but the same shape: setup, inciting incident, rising action, midpoint, crisis, reveal, final image.

Worked example

The 3:40 beat sheet

Setup 0:00-0:34The perfect road
Inciting 0:57The phone buzzes beside the keys
Rising 1:02-1:30She cuts faster; her eyes keep going to the keys
Midpoint 1:30-1:50The near-miss, silence, a thin cut
Crisis 2:02-2:22The light has moved; her hand hovers over the keys
Reveal 2:22-2:35The visor reflects the road: it was the machine
Final image 2:43-3:40She takes the keys and walks out into real dusk
Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write your own seven beats for The Experience Machine with rough times.
  2. For each beat, write what she wants and what blocks her.
  3. Mark the one beat you're least sure of.
What good looks like
  • Every beat is a turn (something changes), not just an event.
  • Times add up to roughly 3-4 minutes.
  • You know your weakest beat and why.
