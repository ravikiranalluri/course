A sound map is a table: rows are beats, columns are ambience bed, middle layer, close detail and silence. It's your score, without music.

Worked example

Two rows

Road: breeze bed, no middle (no footsteps: a clue), hair brush close. Crisis: fridge hum stops, total silence.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Build the sound map for all seven beats.
  2. Mark where silence lands.
  3. Find one sound that works as a setup/payoff.
What good looks like
  • Every beat has all columns filled (or deliberately empty).
  • Silence used two or three times, not everywhere.
  • One sound thread.
