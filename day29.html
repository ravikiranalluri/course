Reconstruct, remix, apply: break down a real piece, recreate it, then change one choice. The elephant reel (from the IDALL course) is short enough to rebuild in a day.

Worked example

A breakdown row

Shot 3, 2.5 s: close-up on the elephant's eye, 85mm, soft backlight, static. Sound: wind bed, a low rumble. Purpose: we're inside its feeling.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Break the reel into a shot list (size, lens, light, move, sound, purpose).
  2. Recreate three shots with stills.
  3. Remix one: change only the light. Note the feeling change.
What good looks like
  • A complete shot list.
  • Three recreated shots close to the originals.
  • A remix with a named feeling change.
