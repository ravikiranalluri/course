A style guide is a one-page promise: palette, light, lenses, texture, framing rules, sound rules. AI drifts; the guide is how you judge drift.

Worked example

Excerpt

Palette: sunset gold, open blue, ember. Light: one motivated hard source. Lenses: 35 wide, 50 mid, 85 close. Texture: grain only in the real world. Framing: road = centered; kitchen = off-center. Sound: no music; three layers plus silence.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write your one-page style guide.
  2. Collect 6 reference frames (your own stills plus films).
  3. Test one new still against the guide.
What good looks like
  • Every rule is checkable (yes or no).
  • References match the rules.
  • The test still passes or you know exactly why not.
