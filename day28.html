Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Read all Week 4 work in one sitting.
  2. Write five rewrite notes.
  3. Produce beat sheet v2.
What good looks like
  • Notes are specific (a beat, a shot, a fix).
  • v2 is shorter or clearer than v1.
  • Nothing in v2 contradicts your theme.
