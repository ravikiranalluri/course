Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

Dialogue before environment. Write the scene as voices only. Your three open questions become the spine: what each shadow wants from the other, the thought the audience leaves holding, and where (village, jail, school, or nowhere specific).

Worked example

A one-page format

Left column: lines of dialogue. Right column: the pause after each line, with a camera move or sound and its reason (power, thought, time). Bottom: the one thought the audience should hold. Location decided last, from the conflict.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write a one-page shadow script in the two-column format.
  2. Decide the location last; write why the conflict chose it.
  3. Read it aloud with the pauses timed.
What good looks like
  • The two wants are clear from the dialogue alone.
  • Every pause has a reason.
  • The location feels like it fell out of the collision.
