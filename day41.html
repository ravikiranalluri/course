Blocking is where people stand and move. In dialogue, movement shows power. Use the Inglourious Basterds idea: the camera and the screen sides follow the power shift.

Worked example

Kitchen at midnight, blocked

Father at the sink, back to her. She stays in the doorway until the turn, then steps in. Camera starts behind her (her POV), ends behind him.
