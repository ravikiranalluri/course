The 12 weeks

From film language basics to your first 2-minute film and beyond. Week 2 is where you are now.

The 12 weeks

W1

Film language basics Done

Shot sizes and angles, reference channels, first topic ideas.

W2

Camera, feeling, and light Now

Camera moves and the Grid (Days 8-9), color-and-music sketch (Day 10), light and camera block (Days 11-14).

W3

Light, color, character

Elephant reel reconstruction, Experience Machine style guide, main character sheet.

W4

Performance, voice, sound

Script and narrate an Experience Machine moment; publish your first reel (30-60 s).

W5-8

The 2-minute film

Script, beat sheet, storyboard, previs, generation and edit of the Experience Machine short with its twist, plus reel cut-downs and one dialogue scene.

W9-12

Going further

Crane, orbit and the impossible mirror shot; 5-8 minute films from your backlog, building toward your 12-minute film.

The light and camera block (Days 11-14)

Day 11

Light as a craft, plus angles

Key, fill and rim light; hard vs soft; direction and time of day. How eye-level, low, high, overhead and Dutch angles change light on a face. Open sky vs overcast vs light through glass.

Day 12

Movement language

Push-through shots (through a doorway, window or object into the scene) and overhead shots (top-down static, crane-down, rotate), built as Blender previs.

Day 13

The open wide-sky look

A Utah-style empty paved road, sky fully exposed with no hills or trees, the road as the only hard edge, hard direct light that reads differently from overcast or glass-filtered light.

Day 14

One combined shot

Light, angle and move combined into one Experience Machine opening, then your first Wan 3.0 video-to-video.

How every lesson works

Reconstruct

Break down a real scene shot by shot, then recreate it.

Remix

Change one director’s choice at a time and notice the feeling change.

Apply

Use it on a piece of your own film.

Taste rep

15 minutes: watch a great scene for feeling, muted, and eyes closed; write three lines.
