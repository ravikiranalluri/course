Use Day 15's method: two wants, the situation falls out, then dialogue where nobody says their want directly. Subtext is the want hiding under the words.

Worked example

Subtext line

Father at the sink (wants to protect her): "It's late. Go to bed." What he means: "Don't ask me about it."

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Pick a seed. Write a one-page scene.
  2. Underline every line where a want hides.
  3. Read it aloud.
What good looks like
  • No one states their want outright.
  • The situation came from the wants.
  • A clear turn by the end.
