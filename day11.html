Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

You said it first: "The light is the subject itself." So learn light's three questions: where is it from, how hard is it, and how many sources?

  • Key: the main light. It decides the mood.
  • Fill: softens the shadows the key makes. Less fill = more drama.
  • Rim (backlight): from behind, outlines hair and shoulders, separates her from the background. Sunset behind her = a natural rim.
  • Hard vs soft: a small, distant source (the sun at sunset, a bare bulb) makes hard, sharp shadows. A big source (overcast sky, a window with sheer curtains) makes soft shadows.
  • Motivated light: every light should have a believable source in the world. In the kitchen, the motivation is the window.
Worked example

Kitchen in three lights

  • Golden sunset through the window (hard, warm, low): a bright beam across the table, long shadows, dust in the air. Feels like a memory, or a perfect moment.
  • Overcast (soft, cool, flat): no beam, gentle shadows, honest. Feels like an ordinary Tuesday. Less magic, more real.
  • Light through frosted glass (soft, diffused, warm): glowing, dreamy, faces wrapped in light. Feels protected, closed in.

Judgment for the film: sunset through the window, because it carries the road's gold inside (the light travels with her). Overcast is held in reserve; it could become the 'real world' look after the twist.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. In Blender, make a grey box: a cube room, a window hole, a table, a simple figure (or use toys and a desk lamp).
  2. Render (or photograph) three stills: low hard sun through the window; a large soft area light (overcast); the same light behind a frosted plane (glass).
  3. For each still, label key, fill and rim (or 'none').
  4. Pick the light for the kitchen pause and write which part of the want it serves.
What good looks like
  • Three stills where the shadows are clearly different (sharp edges vs soft edges).
  • Every light has a motivation you can point to (window, sky, glass).
  • Your pick ties to the want (staying in the perfect evening), not just 'prettiest'.
