Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

The want is what she chases. The need is what she actually has to learn or choose. The flaw keeps her from the need; the wound (never shown) explains the flaw. A short film works when the want and the need pull in opposite directions at the climax.

Worked example

Mara, filled in

  • Want: the evening to stay perfect, and to be left alone inside it.
  • Need: to choose the real, imperfect life where things can go wrong and people need her.
  • Flaw: she prefers a flawless experience to an effortful real one.
  • Wound: she once gave everything to win something and lost.
  • Want-object: her husband's car keys: small, visible, and they force a choice.
Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Fill want, need, flaw, wound and want-object for Mara in your own words (change anything you like).
  2. For each, write where we'd see it on screen without dialogue.
  3. Do the same for one shadow character from Day 17.
What good looks like
  • Want and need clearly point in different directions.
  • Every line has an on-screen moment.
  • The wound stays off-screen but explains a visible behavior.
