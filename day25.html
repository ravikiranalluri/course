Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

Inside each scene ask three questions: what does she want here, what blocks her, what turns? If nothing turns, the scene is a montage, and that's fine only if you meant it.

Worked example

Scene 2, kitchen part 1

Want: finish dinner alone, in control. Obstacle: the phone buzzing; his keys on the table. Turn: the near-miss with the knife. What it creates: the inciting incident and the midpoint.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write want/obstacle/turn for each of your 4 scenes.
  2. Do the same for one of the five famous scenes (Day 15).
  3. Find a scene with no turn and add one.
What good looks like
  • Four scenes, each with a turn you could film.
  • The famous-scene analysis matches what you saw.
  • A turn added where there was none.
