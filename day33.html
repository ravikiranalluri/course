Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

In a wordless film the face is the script. Direct actions, not emotions: "she looks at the keys, then back at the knife, then breathes out" reads better than "she looks conflicted".

Worked example

The pause, directed

"Her hand stops mid-cut. Two seconds still. Her eyes move to the keys without her head turning. A small swallow. She looks back down." Every beat is a visible action.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write the pause as five visible actions.
  2. Act it yourself on camera.
  3. Prompt it for AI and compare.
What good looks like
  • Only actions, no emotion words.
  • Your own take is readable without sound.
  • The AI version keeps at least three of five actions.
