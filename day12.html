Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

Day 8 named the moves. Today is about how they move.

  • Push-through: the camera passes through a barrier (window, doorway, sign) into a space. It says: we're entering someone's private world.
  • Overhead family: top-down static (a map of the moment), crane-down (descending into the moment), rotate (turning above, time or thought turning).
  • Speed: slow moves feel thoughtful; fast moves feel urgent. The same path at half speed is a different emotion.
  • Easing: real cameras accelerate and decelerate. A move that starts and stops at full speed looks robotic. In Blender, keep the default ease-in/ease-out curves; for a sudden stop, cut the ease-out on purpose.

Welles's El Rancho shot is the model push-through: rain, a sign, a skylight, a hidden dissolve, then a lonely woman at a table. We travel from the public world into private misery.

Worked example

Three moves for The Experience Machine (previs)

  • Push-through the kitchen window: from the sunset outside, through the glass, to her at the table. The light enters with us. A bridge from road to kitchen.
  • Overhead on the cutting board: top-down static during the knife rhythm, then a tiny crane-down at the pause until the keys fill more of the frame.
  • Remix by speed: the same push-through at 3 seconds (urgent, intrusive) vs 8 seconds (gentle, dreamy). For her want (a perfect evening), 8 seconds.
Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. In your Blender grey box, animate a camera push-through the window to the table over 8 seconds.
  2. Duplicate it and retime it to 3 seconds. Export both as clips.
  3. Animate an overhead: static for 3 seconds, then a 2-second crane-down toward the keys.
  4. Watch them back to back. Write one feeling word for each, and which one goes in the film.
What good looks like
  • Moves that ease in and out (no robotic starts).
  • The 3-second and 8-second versions feel clearly different to you.
  • Your choice is explained with her want, not with 'it looks smooth'.
