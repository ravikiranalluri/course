Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

The backlog. A channel needs a queue of films so you never start from zero. Twenty topics, each with one image, sorted by how much you want to make it. The Experience Machine is #1; the rest wait their turn (and feed Weeks 11-12).

The Grid (from the IDALL course): upload one strong image at 1:1 and ask an image model for six new 2:3 frames of the same scene, changing only camera placement, angle and composition. You get six angles from one shoot, then you choose. Directing is choosing.

35mm vs 85mm:

  • 35mm shows more world around the face. Slightly wider perspective, more depth in focus. Use it when the place matters: her in the kitchen, the road around her.
  • 85mm flattens and isolates. Background melts into blur; the face is all that matters. Use it for thought and emotion: her face in seconds 5-7.
Worked example

One kitchen frame through the Grid

Start image: her at the wooden table, apron, knife, keys in frame. Grid prompt: "Create six new 2:3 cinematic images based on the reference scene, preserving all characters and props. Only change camera placement, angle, and composition, as alternate photographs from the same professional shoot."

Pick by feeling: the side angle (neutral witness) for the cutting; the overhead (decision) for the keys. Then remix with only the lens changed: the 35mm version shows the kitchen around her (she's in her life); the 85mm version isolates her face (she's in her head). For the pause, 85mm wins.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Finish your 20-topic backlog: topic, one image, one line on the question.
  2. Make or pick one still of the kitchen scene. Run the Grid prompt above at 1:1. Save all six.
  3. Choose the best angle for the near-miss and the best for the keys pause. Write why for each.
  4. Generate the chosen frame twice, once 'shot on 35mm' and once 'shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field'. Compare.
What good looks like
  • 20 rows, each with a picture-able image.
  • Six grid frames that keep the same woman, kitchen and props (if the character changes, re-run with 'preserve all characters' stronger).
  • You can explain your 35 vs 85 choice in terms of the feeling (in her life vs in her head).
