Section 2

Reading

Video (watch) · Exercise (hands-on)

The theme is the film's answer to its question. Write it as one sentence, then check every later choice against it: if a shot doesn't serve the sentence, it goes. The twist is where the theme lands.

Worked example

The Experience Machine's theme

Question: if a machine could give you any experience, would you plug in for life? Theme (example): "A perfect life you only experience is not a life you live; the real one can cut you, and it is the only one that hands you the keys." Link to your message: chase the skill, not the trophy. The road is the trophy; the kitchen is the skill.

Section 3

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Write your theme sentence (one line).
  2. List every shot in your 10-second opening and mark whether it serves the theme.
  3. Weekly critique: pick your best and weakest piece of work from Days 15-20 and write what you'd change.
What good looks like
  • A theme under 30 words that answers the question.
  • At least one shot you'd now cut or change because it doesn't serve the theme.
  • An honest critique with one specific fix.
