Topic 1 · The Experience Machine

The first 10 seconds

Two scenes stitched together: the Utah road is the opening emotional image (wide exterior), and the kitchen is the character’s interior (close contrast). Emotional arc: isolation opening outward, then turning inward.

The breakdown

Seconds1-4The road

Wide exterior

Visual
Utah-style empty paved road, whole sky exposed, no hills or trees; the road is the only hard edge, low horizon. A lone figure far down the road.
Light
Hard direct sunset light; the light itself is the subject; long shadows; reads clearly different from overcast or glass-filtered light.
Sound
No music. Slow summer evening sunset wind from the desert’s temperature difference, a gentle breeze.
Move
She walks toward the camera, into the viewer’s space. Camera static or a very slow push.
Beat
Isolation, opening outward. Immersed in the self; an empty road that shows light, not literally an empty highway.
Seconds5-7Her face

The reveal

Visual
Cut to a close-up of her face, hair slightly covering it in the breeze.
Light
Golden triangle at center, sunset gold wrapping around her, pleasant, not flat.
Sound
The same breeze, closer and softer; maybe the faint brush of hair.
Move
Her index finger slowly clears the hair from her face, a heroine’s entrance.
Beat
The turn from world to person. We meet her.
Seconds8-10The kitchen

Close interior

Visual
Side angle in the kitchen (Monica-style, no island), wooden table, she wears an apron, rapidly cutting vegetables. The knife nearly catches her fingers; she draws her hand back. Her husband’s forgotten car keys are visible on the table.
Light
The same sunset gold, now coming through the kitchen window, carrying the light over from the road.
Sound
Fast knife rhythm, then the near-miss, then the kitchen sound drops to near silence as the pause begins.
Move
Fast hands, a sudden stop, stillness.
Beat
Isolation, turning inward. The pause is where reflection lives, and the keys hold the decision she hasn’t made.

Color script and sound arc

Road: wide gold + open blue
Face: warm gold up close
Kitchen: gold inside walls
  • Color script: wide gold and open blue (road), then warm gold up close (face), then gold inside walls, near-silence (kitchen).
  • Sound arc: breeze, then a closer breeze, then rhythm, then silence. No music anywhere.
  • Open question: is the road real, a memory, or the machine? That choice can carry the twist.

The two working scenes

Opening · wide exterior

The Utah road

An empty road that shows light; whole sky, the road as the only hard edge. A woman walks toward the camera inside a golden triangle of sunset light. Feeling: isolation, immersed in the self.

Contrast · close interior

The kitchen

Like Monica’s from Friends: no island, wooden table, apron. Rapid cutting, a near-miss with the knife, a pause where the sound drops out, and her husband’s forgotten car keys she hasn’t decided whether to take down.
