Silence only works after sound. Build up, then drop. Hold long enough to feel strange (1-3 seconds), then return with one small sound.

Worked example

The crisis silence

Fridge hum, clock, distant traffic → all cut at once → 2 seconds of nothing → a single breath.

Exercise (hands-on)

Video (watch) · Reading

  1. Make three versions of the crisis: 0.5 s, 2 s, 4 s silence.
  2. Watch each.
  3. Pick and justify.
What good looks like
  • Three versions.
  • A choice tied to the feeling.
  • A clean return sound.
